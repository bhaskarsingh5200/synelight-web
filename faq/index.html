General

SYNELIGHT designs and builds connected digital systems: websites, AI solutions, automation and lead generation. Instead of delivering isolated tools, we combine them so your website, AI, automations and CRM work together as one growth system.

Businesses that want to launch, automate or scale digitally — from local companies building their first serious web presence to established businesses adding AI and automation to their operations.

System thinking. Most providers deliver one piece — a website here, ads there. We design the whole flow: how a visitor becomes a lead, how that lead gets qualified automatically, and how your team closes. Technology serves the business, never the reverse.

We work remotely by default, with structured communication at every stage. Discovery calls, reviews and handovers happen over video calls, and everything important is documented in writing.

Website

Yes. Every website is designed around your business goals — business websites, landing pages, e-commerce, web applications, admin dashboards and CMS setups. No recycled templates.

A focused landing page can be ready within days. A full business website typically takes a few weeks, depending on scope, content readiness and feedback rounds. You get a clear timeline after discovery.

Yes — mobile is designed intentionally, not shrunk from desktop. Layouts, navigation and forms are rebuilt for small screens, with touch-friendly targets and fast load times.

We set up deployment, hosting configuration and domain connection as part of every web project, and hand over full ownership of everything we create.

AI

Yes — chat assistants, lead qualifiers, workflow agents and voice agents for calls. Each agent is scoped to a concrete business task with clear boundaries, so it behaves predictably.

Practical things: respond to inquiries instantly, qualify leads before your team engages, summarize conversations, draft follow-ups, route requests and handle repetitive communication. The value is in removing waiting time and manual work.

Not when designed properly. AI handles the first response and routine steps instantly, then hands warm, qualified conversations to you or your team. Customers get faster answers; humans handle the moments that matter.

Automation

In most cases, yes. We map how work currently flows through your business, identify repetitive steps, and automate them with integrations, AI workflows and CRM automation while keeping approvals where you want control.

We connect systems through APIs and webhooks — CRMs, WhatsApp Business, forms, spreadsheets, email, payment tools and custom databases. If a tool has an API, it can usually join the system.

Automations are built with error handling, retries and notifications, so failures are surfaced instead of silent. Ongoing maintenance arrangements include monitoring and fixes.

Lead Generation

It's a pipeline where AI captures incoming interest, qualifies it against your criteria, scores it and triggers follow-up — so your team spends time only on opportunities worth pursuing.

From channels you already have or we build for you: your website, landing pages, campaigns, WhatsApp and social media. The system captures and processes whatever enters it.

Each lead is evaluated on signals you define — intent, budget range, urgency, fit. High-scoring leads are routed to sales immediately; others enter automated nurturing until they're ready.

Calling Agents

A voice agent that holds natural phone conversations: it answers inbound calls or places outbound ones, qualifies the caller, books appointments and logs everything to your CRM.

Yes. The agent checks real availability in your calendar and books slots during the call, sending confirmations automatically.

The agent handles first conversations and routine qualification. Complex questions, negotiations and closing stay human — the agent hands those calls over with full context attached.

Social Media

Strategy, content planning, creation (including reels), publishing schedules, community management and monthly analytics reporting — consistent presence without your team carrying the load.

Primarily Instagram and LinkedIn — the two platforms where most businesses see real returns. Platform selection always follows where your audience actually is.

Maintenance

Yes. Systems need care to stay reliable — updates, monitoring, fixes and iterative improvements. We offer ongoing arrangements sized to how much your system evolves.
