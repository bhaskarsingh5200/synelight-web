Privacy Policy.

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what information SYNELIGHT collects through this website and during client engagements, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to synelight.com and any communication with us through forms, email or social channels.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: when you submit a contact or project form, we receive the details you provide — typically your name, business name, contact details and a description of your needs.
  • Communication records: emails, WhatsApp messages and call notes related to your inquiry or project are retained for continuity of service.
  • Basic technical data: like most websites, our hosting may process standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for security and reliability.

We do not buy, sell or enrich data from third-party data brokers.

2. How we use it

  • Responding to inquiries and preparing project proposals
  • Delivering contracted services and ongoing support
  • Sending occasional updates you have asked for (you can opt out anytime)
  • Maintaining the security and performance of this website

3. Client systems and third parties

Projects may involve third-party platforms we configure on your behalf — hosting providers, CRMs, messaging tools, AI service providers and payment processors. Each processes data under its own terms; we select reputable providers and configure them responsibly as part of delivery. We never place your customer data into systems without documenting what they are and why they're there.

4. AI processing

Where our services use AI features (for example, automated responses or calling agents), we configure them to process only the information needed for the task at hand, under the client's instructions. We don't use your confidential business data to train public models, and we recommend appropriate provider settings for every deployment.

5. Cookies & analytics

This website is intentionally lightweight and sets no advertising cookies. If you accept analytics ("Accept" in the consent banner), we load Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate usage — pages visited, rough geography, device type. Analytics only loads after your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time by clearing this site's storage in your browser settings. If you decline, no analytics data is collected.

6. Retention

Inquiry records are kept as long as needed to serve you and satisfy legal or accounting obligations. You can ask us to delete correspondence we hold about you at any time, subject to those obligations.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, contact us via the details below. We respond within a reasonable timeframe.

8. Security

We apply sensible technical measures — encrypted transport, access control and least-privilege defaults — appropriate to a business of our size and the sensitivity of the data involved. No system is perfectly secure, but we design so that a compromise exposes as little as possible.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the revision date above will be updated and significant changes will be noted on this page.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through our contact page. We take every request seriously.
