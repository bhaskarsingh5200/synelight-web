02 · AI Automation

Turn repetitive work into automated systems.

Every manual handoff in your business is a delay waiting to happen. We build automations that move data, qualify leads and trigger actions — while you focus on running the business.

Start This Project → Talk on WhatsApp → Automate My Business → See a Workflow →

Sound familiar?

Leads wait too long

Inquiries sit in an inbox until someone finds time. By then, interest has cooled or a competitor answered first.

Data lives everywhere

Contact details in chats, quotes in spreadsheets, history in someone's memory. Nothing connects.

Manual busywork compounds

Copy-pasting between tools, re-typing orders, chasing confirmations — hours that never appear on any invoice but cost real money.

Scope

What can be automated

  • Lead intake & routing — every inquiry captured, structured and delivered to the right place instantly
  • CRM updates — records created, statuses changed, activity logged without typing
  • WhatsApp conversations — instant replies, confirmations and follow-up sequences
  • Follow-up sequences — timed, personalized touches that keep opportunities warm
  • Data workflows — between spreadsheets, forms, invoices and internal tools
  • Notifications & alerts — your team knows what matters the moment it happens

Principles we automate by

  1. Automate visible work

    You should always see what the system did and when — no black boxes.

  2. Keep humans in command

    Approvals and overrides stay where you want them.

  3. Fail loudly, not silently

    Error handling and alerts are part of every workflow.

  4. Start small, prove value

    First automation should save time within days — then scale.
