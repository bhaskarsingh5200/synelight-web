The System

Five components working together.

01 — LEAD CAPTURE

Capture everything

Landing pages, website forms, WhatsApp and campaigns all feed one pipeline. No inquiry is ever lost in an inbox.

02 — QUALIFICATION

Qualify automatically

AI asks the right questions and evaluates answers against your criteria before a human ever gets involved.

03 — SCORING

Score every opportunity

Leads are ranked by intent, urgency and fit — so your team's attention goes to the best opportunities first.

04 — FOLLOW-UP

Follow up in seconds

Instant responses and timed sequences keep leads warm. Speed matters — interest fades fast.

05 — CRM

Synchronize your CRM

Every interaction, score and status lands in your CRM automatically. One source of truth for sales.

06 — ANALYTICS

See what works

Where leads come from, which convert, where they drop off — decisions based on data instead of guesses.

Why most lead flow leaks.

  • Responses take hours or days instead of seconds
  • No one knows which inquiries are worth priority
  • Follow-up depends on someone remembering
  • Data never reaches the CRM completely
  • Nobody can say which channel produces real customers

What changes with a system

  1. Speed

    Every lead gets an immediate response — day, night, weekend.

  2. Focus

    Your team works scored opportunities, not raw inquiries.

  3. Consistency

    The same quality of follow-up happens every single time.

  4. Clarity

    You finally see the full journey from click to customer.
