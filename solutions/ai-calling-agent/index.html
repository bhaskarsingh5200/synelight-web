How AI calling works.

The agent speaks, listens and acts — following a script you control, with boundaries you define.

Inbound calls

Every incoming call gets answered instantly — questions answered, intent understood, next steps taken. No more missed-call losses.

Outbound calls

The agent reaches out: new leads contacted within minutes, no-shows re-booked, quotes followed up — automatically.

Lead qualification

Natural questions uncover need, timing and fit. The caller is scored before your team picks anything up.

Appointment booking

Real calendar availability is checked live during the call, slots are booked and confirmations sent on the spot.

Follow-up

Promised callbacks actually happen. Sequenced follow-up keeps opportunities moving without manual reminders.

Human handover

Complex or high-value conversations transfer to your team with full context attached — nothing starts from zero.
